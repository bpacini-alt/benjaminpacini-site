Read the Relationship

You may know which way the alligator faces. Now practice reading the comparison the symbol actually makes.

Practice and discussion. These rounds are not graded, and responses are not saved. The alligator can help you remember where to place a sign. The words less than and greater than tell you what the sign means.
Extra practice

Choose a short round from the item bank for the selected tier.

Read from left to right

The sign compares the value on its left with the value on its right.

6 < 7 means 6 is less than 7
9 > 4 means 9 is greater than 4

Then decide whether the statement is true. Reading it correctly and judging it correctly are two different steps.

One useful check

Cover the symbol and read the comparison as a sentence. Start with the number on the left; keep the numbers in that order as you read.

Teaching lens

What did the first prompt tell you?

The opening item checked whether you could place a sign in a familiar format. The next item asked you to read and evaluate the relationship.

  1. What could a student do on the first prompt without knowing what the sign says?
  2. What clear explanation would connect the symbol with the words “less than” or “greater than”?
  3. What different question would show that students can read and use the relationship?
